# Global coverage and cost estimate

Status: not estimated yet. No global investor total or budget is claimed until public sources and pilot effort measurements are collected.

The estimate will separate VC, private equity, family offices, angel investors, and large private investors. For each category and region, record the source, its definition and date, observed count, likely overlap, public-data limits, and lower/base/upper scenario. Do not treat association membership as the total market or extrapolate Czech VC disclosure rates to other categories.

Cost components: engineering, candidate discovery, public-source review, AI extraction if used, human verification, infrastructure, and recurring updates. Human review cost = candidates × review share × minutes per review / 60 × hourly rate. Rates and tariffs will be cited with access dates; assumptions will be labeled.
